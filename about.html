Guest Guidelines & Terms

T & H Inn Property Policies

Official house rules, check-in requirements, and guest guidelines for a safe and comfortable stay.

Verified Property Standards
Check-In / Check-Out
Standard Check-In 1:00 AM
Standard Check-Out 11:00 AM

Early check-in or late check-out requests are subject to room availability upon arrival.

Guest ID & Verification
  • Primary guest must be at least 18 years of age.
  • Indian Guests: Mandatory Aadhaar Card (Driving License, Passport, or Govt ID accepted).
  • International Guests: Original Passport and valid VISA are mandatory.
Alcohol & Quiet Hours
  • Alcohol consumption is permitted inside rooms only when consumed responsibly.
  • Please maintain noise control to respect neighboring guests.
  • The homestay does not sell or supply alcoholic beverages.
Child & Extra Bed
  • 2 Kids (aged 6 to 12 years) are considered as 1 adult guest.
  • An extra bed can be provided for additional children or adults at a nominal charge.
Pet Restrictions
  • Pets are not allowed anywhere on the property premises.
  • Ensures a clean, allergen-free environment for all resident guests.
Parking & Accessibility
  • Free on-site car parking is available for staying guests.
  • Ground floor and easy stairwell access to all room levels.
